# Melina Mode — Design System

## Product
Melina Mode: women's fashion brand (modest/elegant ready-to-wear — linen, silk, soft neutrals). Single-page brand/shop landing: hero (video banner + logo badge), about ("Quiet elegance" + palette), collections (2 cards), contact form, footer. GA4 tracked.

## Color (hard constraint)
- Blush pink #F8DDE3 (badge, cards, accents) · badge border #E7BFC8
- Black #1A1A1A (text, primary buttons)
- Beige/sand #C4A78F (accents, linen card, underline, heart)
- White #FFFFFF · Page background cream #FAF8F3
- Light theme only. No dark mode, no neon, no gradients except soft blurred blush/beige blobs and a dark overlay on the hero video.

## Typography
- Script: **Allura** (logo "Melina", large section headings) — normal case, very large (text-6xl–8xl), leading-none
- Sans: **Montserrat** 300/400/500/600 — body, nav, buttons; "MODE" in 600 weight with tracking 0.35em
- Small uppercase labels use tracking-widest

## Shape & effects
- Pill buttons/inputs (rounded-full); cards rounded-3xl, aspect 4/5; soft shadows (shadow-xl); backdrop-blur on nav after scroll
- Linen texture on beige card (subtle repeating lines)
- Motion: fade/translate reveal on scroll (0.8s), soft float on blobs, slight card parallax, hover scale 1.05 on dark buttons

## Logo
Pink circle (#F8DDE3, 6px border #E7BFC8), script "Melina" with small beige heart at upper-right, spaced "MODE" below, short beige (#C4A78F) bar underneath. Small version (script + MODE) in nav.

## Voice
English, short, calm, elegant. "Quiet elegance", "Silk & Blush", "Linen Edit".
